Apparatus criticus

The text is reproduced in the painted inscription on the wall blocking the arcosolium niche. This inscription was probably painted in the early 19th century. The 19th-century inscription is edited by Theocharis Pazaras, whereas the philological edition provided here follows the edition by Andreas Rhoby. We prefer the latter, since the original Byzantine version is assumed to be lost and we treat the text as a reconstruction. A. Rhoby has corrected line 4 of the 19th-century copy from ἀγαποίσας to ἀγαπούσας.
