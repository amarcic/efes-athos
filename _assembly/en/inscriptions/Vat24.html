Apparatus criticus

line 5: Dimitris Liakos restores the second digit of the year as ρ=100, which would give a date after 1592 (=anno mundi 7100). This is incompatible with the known dates of the superiorship of the abbot Maximos referred to in the text (1563-1568). Therefore, the restoration proposed here is ο=70 (i.e., 707X), which generates a date ranging from 1562 to 1571. The third digit must have been α, β, γ, δ, ε, or ς (i.e., 7071-7076 = 1563-1568). In any case, it is clear that this was a three-digit number (with a place value of 0 hundreds).
