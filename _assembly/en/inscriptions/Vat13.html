Apparatus criticus

The original inscription is no longer extant. The text has been reproduced as the first part of the dedicatory inscription referring to maintenance works and repairs to the structure of the outer narthex in 1819. The reconstruction of the text is reliable, since it is also copied in manuscript Vatopedi, cod. 1037 (fol. 260r), which dates from the 18th century (i.e., before the renovation and repainting of the outer narthex) and therefore appears to transmit the text of the original inscription, although there are differences between the two versions. The transcription in the manuscript reads: Ἀνιστορήθη ὁ παρὼν ἱερὸς καὶ θεῖος ναὸς οὗτος, παρὰ τῆς βασιλείας Ἀνδρονίκου τοῦ ὀρθοδοξωτάτου Δεσπότου Κομνηνοῦ, τοῦ Παλαιολόγου, διά συνδρομῆς κυρίου Αρσενίου, ἐν ἔτει 6820, ἀπὸ δὲ τῆς ἐνσάρκου οἰκονομίας 1312, ἰνδικτιῶνος δεκάτης.
