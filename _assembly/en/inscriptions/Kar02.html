Commentary

If the conversion of the calendar is accurate, this means that the date falls between January and August 1592: the year 7100 anno mundi corresponds to September 1591 - August 1592; within this time frame the only months that overlap with the anno domini date given (1592) are January-August. It is known however that inscriptions containing dates in both calendars often make mistakes.
