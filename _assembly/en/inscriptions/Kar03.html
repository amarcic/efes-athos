The date 1687 was recorded by Uspenskij; it was most likely given in Greek numerals. It is possible (but not very likely) that it was originally a date anno mundi and that Uspenskij converted it into anno domini, in which case it would have read 7195 or 7196 (ζρϞε/ζρϞς in Greek).

Commentary

The date referred either to the date of construction or to the fresco decoration.
