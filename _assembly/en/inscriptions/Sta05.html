Apparatus criticus

The first lines of the inscription are not preserved, but the text is attested by Uspenskij in 1846, who must have seen it before the remodelling of the south side of the main church. The missing part of the original text appears to have mentioned that the church was constructed thanks to the patronage of patriarch Jeremias I and painted by Theophanes the Cretan and Simeon, with the contribution of Makarios, bishop of Hierissos and Mount Athos, and Gregory, dikaios of the monastery, on 3 July. Since there is not enough space for all this information on the mullion capital, the first lines of the text must have been inscribed elsewhere, perhaps onto the masonry above the capital, between the two openings of the original bifora window.
