Mt Athos Epigraphy

This project will produce the first comprehensive corpus of donor inscriptions from all twenty Athonite monasteries, as well as from the Protaton, the peninsula’s central administrative church. Chronologically, the study spans from the 10th to the 17th centuries. This period encompasses the formation of the monastic communities and their development under two successive imperial frameworks: Byzantine and Ottoman.
