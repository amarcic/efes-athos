Dedicatory inscription of icon frame for the icon of the Virgin Esphagmene

Description: On the icon frame (koubouklion) for the icon of the Virgin Esphagmene

Text: description of text field/campus

Letters: letter-heights

Date: Unknown.

Findspot: Unknown

Original location: Vatopedi, Chapel of Saint Demetrios

Last recorded location: attested, lost (Inv. no. inventory number)

Interpretive

7189 = ἀπό δὲ Χ(ριστ)οῦ ἔτος 1681. Ἡγουμενεύοντος τοῦ πανοσιωτάτου κυρίου Σεραφείμ ἔγινε τὸ παρὸν διὰ συνδρομῆς καὶ ἐξόδου, τοῦ ὁσιωτάτου ἐν Μοναχοῖς, γέρο κυρ Ἀνθίμου τὸ ἐπίκλην Ὀζαδίνου, και γέροντος τῆς Μονῆς τοῦ βατοπαιδίου, χεὶρ δὲ Μακαρίου ἱερομονάχου, ζωγράφου Καλλέργη.

Diplomatic

Apparatus criticus

The text is transmitted by Proskynetarion 293. The transcription offered here follows that of Ioakeim Papangelos. It appears that the phrase ἀπό δὲ Χ(ριστ)οῦ ἔτος 1681 is an addition by the scribe of the manuscript and that the inscription only included the date from Creation, 7189. In this case, the reckoning 1681 would have to be corrected to 1680/1681, since there is no indication of month or that the scribe was aware of the complete conversion formula X-5509/5508.
