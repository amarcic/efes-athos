Dedicatory inscription of the iconostasis Crucifix

Description: Painted on the Crucifix of the iconostasis

Text: The text is inscribed on both sides of the figure of Christ

Letters: Majuscules with occasional accents and breathings; ligatures letter-heights

Date: 17th century

Findspot: Unknown

Original location: Vatopedi, Main church

Last recorded location: Now preserved in the outer narthex. (Inv. no. inventory number)
